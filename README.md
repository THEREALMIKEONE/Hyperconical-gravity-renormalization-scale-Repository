# Hyperconical gravity applied to the renormalization scale

Mike One, 2026. Paper 5 of the scale-axis series: a prototype that reads the cone of hyperconical modified gravity (HMG, R. Monjo) as time and as scale, and places both readings on one sheet (S, t).

- `Hyperconical_gravity_applied_to_the_renormalization_scale_Mike_One.pdf`: the paper (13 pages)
- `paper5_source.zip`: its LaTeX source and figures
- `hmg_scale_axis/`: the scripts behind every number in the paper, with their outputs; see its own README

## What the paper does

- HMG's universe is the edge of the sheet; a cut at one time gives every layer of structure, from the floor to our layer; a cut at one layer gives its history.
- Four force layers (weak, strong, electromagnetic, gravity), each with its own vacuum; the vacuum changes sign at the floor and at our layer, and the scale axis continues past both.
- Where the edge meets our layer, with nothing fitted: HMG's vacuum 0.694 ρ_crit against 0.697 ± 0.004 measured; its Jeans rate gives the galactic scale g† = 1.06e-10 m/s², which is HMG's a = 2c/(γ₀t) with γ₀ = 12.3; isolated KiDS-1000 lenses read it directly.
- Predictions: the galactic scale stays constant within 1% to z = 2; the vacuum's equation of state never crosses −1.
